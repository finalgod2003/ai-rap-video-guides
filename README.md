## How to choose without buying the wrong workflow

Choose by the asset you want to preserve:

- **The recording:** use a tool that accepts your audio. Ask whether it animates a visible performer, makes background visuals, or does both.
- **The idea:** start with a scene-led or topic-led generator. Keep the brief to one setting, one action and one short message.
- **The exact words:** inspect the lyric controls before paying. A prompt mentioning a sentence does not guarantee that every word will be delivered correctly.
- **Two recognizable people:** check whether the tool needs two separate portraits or accepts a shared photo. These inputs are not interchangeable across products.

Use the following comparison brief when you try tools yourself. Keep the portrait, framing and idea consistent where the tools allow it:

> One adult performer in a warm studio, facing the camera. A steady medium shot with a slow push-in. Relaxed hand gestures and one continuous performance. Original upbeat rap vocals with bass and drums. No scene changes or on-screen text.

That is a starting brief, not a test result. For an audio-upload tool, use the same recording you own and choose the same passage. For a tool that generates its own audio, judge whether the mood fits the brief rather than expecting it to reproduce that recording.

Before a larger purchase, inspect the face, hands, mouth movement, words, sound and crop in the preview. Write down the **amount you must actually pay to download one usable result**. Include the minimum pack purchase and any retries you choose to make, rather than treating unlike credit systems as equivalent.

## FAQ

### What is an AI rap video generator?

It is a tool that creates a rap-style performance video or makes visuals for rap audio. Some start with a portrait and generate the performance and sound together; others need an existing track. Check the required input first, because the same label covers different workflows.

### Which option should I choose for two people?

For Rap AI Video, start from [Hotel Lobby](https://rapaivideo.io/hotel-lobby-ai-video?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai_rap_video_guides&utm_content=description_link) or the studio duo case and upload two separate photos. RapVideo.ai also offers a duo workflow. Choose between them by whether you want a scene-led performance or the audio choices described on its product page.

### Can I upload my own song to Rap AI Video?

No. Rap AI Video generates audio as part of the result and does not accept song or voice uploads. If preserving a recording is essential, inspect the audio-led tools above. Our [guide to making a rap song with AI](https://rapaivideo.io/how-to-make-a-rap-song-with-ai?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai_rap_video_guides&utm_content=description_link) explains what our short-video workflow can do.

### Does a free preview include the download?

Not necessarily. On Rap AI Video, the free generation previews the first 5 seconds, while full playback and download require a purchase. Other tools may offer a watermarked video, a lyric preview or a different trial. Read what is included before treating an offer as a free export.

### What should I check before sharing?

Use photos and recordings you have permission to use. Review the generated words, likeness and audio, and check the publishing platform's current AI-labeling requirements. A generator's template does not by itself establish permission to reuse a third-party song or imply an artist's endorsement.
